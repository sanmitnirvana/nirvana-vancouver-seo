10 / Common Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is an ongoing process. Technical fixes may be reflected relatively quickly, while meaningful growth in competitive organic searches can take several months or longer. Timing depends on your website, competition, content and execution.

How much do Vancouver SEO services cost?

Pricing varies based on your goals, website complexity, market competition, target locations and required content or development work. We recommend an assessment and tailored scope before quoting.

Can SEO help my Google Maps visibility?

Local SEO can improve the accuracy and relevance of your Google Business Profile, location information and supporting website content. Google controls rankings, so specific positions cannot be guaranteed.

Does Nirvana Canada handle technical website fixes?

Nirvana Canada has web-development capabilities alongside SEO expertise. We can evaluate technical implementation needs and clarify which fixes are included in your agreed scope.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No responsible agency can guarantee specific organic rankings. Our approach focuses on sound technical work, useful content, transparent reporting and measurable business outcomes.

Can SEO improve visibility in AI-powered search?

Clear, accurate content and accessible page structures can make information easier for search and AI systems to understand. Inclusion or citation in AI-generated answers cannot be guaranteed.

How will I know whether SEO is generating leads?

Where feasible, we configure or review conversion tracking for forms, calls and other important actions, then connect these metrics to search visibility and traffic reports. Attribution has limitations that we explain.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. An SEO engagement can begin with an audit of your existing site and a prioritized plan for improvements. A full website redesign is not always necessary.
